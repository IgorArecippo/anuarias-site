Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Once Twice Melody" is the eighth studio album by Beach House. It was released in four chapters starting November 10, 2021, with the entire album being released in full on February 18, 2022, through Sub Pop Records. A follow-up of their seventh studio album 7 (2018), they began recording the album in 2018 at their home studio, Apple Orchard Studio, in Baltimore, with additional recording taking place at Pachyderm Studio, United Studio and Village Recorders until July 2021. It is the first album to be produced soley and entirely by the band. The album was mostly mixed by Alan Moulder…
