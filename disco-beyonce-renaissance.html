Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Renaissance" (also referred to as "Act I: Renaissance") is the seventh studio album by American singer, songwriter, and producer Beyoncé. It was released on 29 July 2022 by Parkwood Entertainment and Columbia Records as the first part of a planned trilogy. Guest vocalists on the album include Beam, Grace Jones, and Tems. Beyoncé developed and recorded "Renaissance" during the COVID-19 pandemic with the intention of inspiring joy and escapism following a period of collective isolation.
