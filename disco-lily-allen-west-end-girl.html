Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

“West End Girl” is the fifth studio album by English singer songwriter Lily Allen. It was released on 24 October 2025 through BMG. The album is her first release in over 7 years, following 2018s No Shame. Written and recorded in Los Angeles over sixteen days in December 2024, the album explores the undoing of Allen's marriage to Stranger Things actor David Harbour, and how she processed his alleged acts of infidelity.
