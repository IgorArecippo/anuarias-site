Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

To Feel Alive (stylized in all caps) is the second EP by Kali Uchis, released on April 24, 2020, through Virgin EMI Records and Interscope Records. Unable to issue her second studio album, the singer recorded the EP's songs in her home while self-isolating due to the COVID-19 pandemic. It is her first extended play since Por Vida and first release overall since Isolation.
