Do disco

  1. É um disco conceitual contra o consumismo e "o lado feio do capitalismo"; Mark Foster o chamou de "uma conversa com Deus".
  2. Quase se chamou A Beginner's Guide to Destroying the Moon ou Pseudologia Fantastica — nomes difíceis demais de lembrar, que viraram faixas.
  3. Pra divulgar, a banda pintou um mural de 40 metros em Los Angeles, o maior da costa oeste dos EUA.
  4. As influências foram Clash, Kinks, David Bowie e música da África Ocidental.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Supermodel is the second studio album by American indie rock band Foster the People. The album was co-produced by their frontman Mark Foster and British musician Paul Epworth, and was released by Columbia Records on March 14, 2014 in Friday-release countries and March 18, 2014 in the United States. It is the follow-up to the band's 2011 debut album, Torches.
