Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Trava Línguas is the second studio album by Brazilian singer-songwriter Linn da Quebrada, released on July 16, 2021, through Natura Musical. The album was entirely produced by BadSista and features collaborations with Brazilian singers Luísa Nascim and Ventura Profana.
