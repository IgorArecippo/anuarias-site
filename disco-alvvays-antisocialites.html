Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Antisocialites is the Canadian indie pop band Alvvays' second album released on September 8, 2017 via Polyvinyl, Royal Mountain, Transgressive and Inertia. The album was produced by John Congleton and was promoted with the singles "In Undertow", "Dreams Tonite" and "Plimsoll Punks". The album was recorded using Pro Tools and a Tascam 488 8-track recorder. The album was recorded in multiple studios, including Kingsize Soundlabs in Los Angeles, California, Tarbox Road Studios in Buffalo, New York and Union Sound Studios in Toronto, Canada.
