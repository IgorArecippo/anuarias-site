Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Gemini Rights is Steve Lacy’s second studio album, and fourth project overall, following his 2020 album The Lo-Fis. It was released on July 15, 2022, by RCA Records. Steve Lacy first announced the album on Instagram on February 18, 2022.
