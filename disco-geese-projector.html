Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Projector is the second studio album and label debut by American indie rock band Geese. The album was released on October 29, 2021, through Partisan Records and Play It Again Sam. The album had three singles released ahead of the release: "Disco", "Low Era", and "Projector". The album received widespread critical acclaim upon its release. Recording for Projector began in 2019 and continued in 2020 and was primarily recorded in drummer Max Bassin's basement from late 2019 to early 2020, and mixed by Dan Carey.
