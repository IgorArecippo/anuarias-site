Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Sin Miedo (del Amor y Otros Demonios) ∞ is the second studio album and first Spanish album by Colombian-American singer Kali Uchis, released on November 18, 2020, through Interscope Records and EMI Records. The title of the album is a reference to the 1994 novel Of Love and Other Demons by the Colombian author Gabriel García Márquez. The album, which is Uchis' first record predominantly sung in Spanish, was supported by three singles, "Aquí Yo Mando", "La Luz" and "Telepatía".
