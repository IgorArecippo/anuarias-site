Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Prelude to Ecstasy is the debut album by British alternative rock band The Last Dinner Party, released on 2 February 2024 by Island Records. It was produced by James Ford, and includes the singles "Nothing Matters", "Sinner", "My Lady of Mercy", "On Your Side" and "Caesar on a TV Screen". The band will tour the UK and Europe from January to July 2024 in support of the album. The album received acclaim from critics.
