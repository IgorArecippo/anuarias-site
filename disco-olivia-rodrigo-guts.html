Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

GUTS is the second studio album by American singer-songwriter Olivia Rodrigo. It was released on September 8, 2023, Via Geffen Records/Interscope Records. It was preceded by its lead single "Vampire", released on June 30, 2023. It debuted atop the Billboard Hot 100, scoring Rodrigo her third number-one single in the United States. The second single, "bad idea right?", was released on August 11, 2023. GUTS was released on September 8, 2023, via CD, cassette, streaming platforms, and three box set editions.
