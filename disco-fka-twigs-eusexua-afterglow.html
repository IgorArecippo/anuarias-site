Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

EUSEXUA Afterglow is the fourth studio album by English singer-songwriter, dancer, actress, and record producer FKA twigs. It was released on November 14, 2025, through Young and Atlantic Records. The album was preceded by the singles "Cheap Hotel", released on September 25. An accompanying music video was released for the single; The second single from the album, "Predictable Girl", was announced across Twigs’ social media profiles, with the single and its music video being released concurrently on October 11.
