Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Equus Caballus is the sixth studio album by Canadian indie-pop band Men I Trust, released on May 6, 2025. The record was issued as a companion to Equus Asinus, released earlier the same year, forming a two-part project. The album contains 13 tracks and features previously released singles such as «Billie Toppy», «Ring of Past», and «Husk», alongside new material.
