Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

The Lead is FLO's debut extended play which released on July 8, 2022. It features the hit-single "Cardboard Box" and "Immature". A sequential re-release of "The Lead" on September 21, 2022 added the single "Not My Job".
