Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Clancy is the seventh LP and fifth studio album by American musical duo Twenty One Pilots. It was released on May 24, 2024, through Fueled by Ramen. A concept record titled after the protagonist of the duo's fifth LP Trench (2018), Clancy continues the titular character's story, as established in Trench and their sixth LP, Scaled and Icy (2021). The album's lead single, "Overcompensate", was released on February 29, 2024.
