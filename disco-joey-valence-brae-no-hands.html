Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

NO HANDS is the second independent album from alternative rap duo Joey Valence & Brae. The title of this album is a double entendre playing on the concept of “no hands” meaning to do something effortlessly and skillfully that you don’t even need hands, but also doing so with no “hands” as in violence – indicated by JVB’s shirts in the album cover. Lyrically this album explores concepts from confidence, rebellion, violence, and authenticity.
