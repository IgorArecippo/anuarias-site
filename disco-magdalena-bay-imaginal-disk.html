Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Imaginal Disk is the second studio album by American duo Magdalena Bay, released on August 23, 2024, through Mom + Pop Music. The title shares its name with imaginal discs, the structures within the body of insect larvae that form part of their adult bodies. For the album, Magdalena Bay reimagined the term as a CD-shaped object created by aliens that, once inserted into the brain, gives the being "upgraded consciousness". In the story, the technology was inserted into the brains of apes to make humans.
