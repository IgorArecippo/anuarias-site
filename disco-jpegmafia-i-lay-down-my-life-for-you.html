Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

I LAY DOWN MY LIFE FOR YOU is the fifth studio album by JPEGMAFIA, released in August 1st 2024. The album was first teased in January, 2023, where Peggy revealed he had three studio albums for 2023. The first would see its release in March, the Danny Brown collab, Scaring the Hoes. Peggy initially planned to drop the album on January 2, 2024, after the release of The Ghost~Pop Tape (Remastered + Original), which served as the second release of the trilogy.
