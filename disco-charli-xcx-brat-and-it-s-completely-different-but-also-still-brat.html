Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

brat and it’s completely different but also still brat (stylized all in lowercase) is the reissue of the sixth studio album by singer Charli xcx or also known as the remix album of BRAT. The record includes remixes for all of songs on the standard edition, as well as of the deluxe tracks «Guess» and «Spring breakers», with the full unremixed deluxe edition of BRAT tacked on to the end.
