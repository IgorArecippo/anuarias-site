Do disco

  1. Will Toledo lançou o disco sozinho no Bandcamp em 2011; foi baixado por umas 100 pessoas no começo e passou de 33 mil downloads em 2018.
  2. Virou cult graças ao 4chan e ao Reddit.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Twin Fantasy is the sixth album by American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist Will Toledo under the Car Seat Headrest moniker. Originally released on November 2nd, 2011, the album was recorded entirely by Toledo during his first semester at The College of William and Mary, inspired by a relationship the 19-year-old was in at the time. In subsequent years, Twin Fantasy would go on to become a cult hit, gaining traction through internet forums like 4chan and Reddit.
