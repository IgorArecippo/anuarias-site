Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Bando Stone and the New World is the fifth studio album by American rapper Donald Glover, under the stage name Childish Gambino, and the soundtrack album from his upcoming film of the same name. It was released on July 19, 2024, through RCA Records, and is Glover's final album under the Childish Gambino moniker.
