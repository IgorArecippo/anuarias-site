Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

CHROMAKOPIA is the eighth studio album by American rapper and producer Tyler, The Creator. Released on October 28, 2024 through Columbia Records, CHROMAKOPIA was released three years after his previous album, CALL ME IF YOU GET LOST. The album contains features from artists such as Daniel Caesar, Teezo Touchdown, Childish Gambino, GloRilla, Lil Wayne, Sexyy Red, LaToiya Williams, ScHoolboy Q, Santigold, Lola Young, and Doechii, and the production is handled by himself.
