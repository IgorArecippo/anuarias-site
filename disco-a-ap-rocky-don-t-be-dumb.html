Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

“Don't Be Dumb” is the fourth studio album by American rapper and record producer ASAP Rocky. It released on January 16, 2026, through AWGE / ASAP Worldwide and RCA Records. The album features contributions from Brent Faiyaz, Danny Elfman, Doechii, Gorillaz, Sauce Walka, Slay Squad, Thundercat, Tyler, the Creator, Westside Gunn, and will.i.am, among others. It's his first studio album in almost 8 years, since his third studio album Testing (2018). The album was released on National Nothing Day in the United States.
