Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

MOTOMAMI is the third studio album by Spanish singer-songwriter Rosalía, released on March 18, 2022 through Columbia Records. The concept album features guest vocals from The Weeknd and Tokischa. Four singles were released from the album, with the lead single "La Fama (with The Weeknd)" peaking at number two on the US Hot Latin Songs chart, and reaching the top spot on the Spanish charts, marking Rosalía's eighth number-one single in the country.
