Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

That! Feels Good! is the fifth studio album by British singer Jessie Ware, released on 28 April, 2023. The album follows her critically-acclaimed fourth studio album, What's Your Pleasure?. It features the singles Free Yourself, Pearls and Begin Again. The album was announced on 9 February, 2023 with the release of the second single, Pearls.
