Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"COWBOY CARTER" (Act II) is the eighth studio album by American singer and songwriter Beyoncé. It was released on 29 March 2024 through Parkwood Entertainment and Columbia Records. The album is a concept work and the second part of a planned trilogy following "Renaissance" (2022). It was conceived as a reimagining of Americana, highlighting the often overlooked contributions of Black pioneers to American music and cultural history. The album is rooted in country music and incorporates a range of styles from the Southern United States, reflecting Beyoncé’s Texas upbringing.
