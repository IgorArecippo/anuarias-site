Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Songs of Her's is a nine-track compilation of songs by Liverpool duo, Stephen Fitzpatrick and Audun Laading, namely Her's, released on 12th May 2017. The compilation is the band's work over the first twelve months, but not released as their debut album. The compilation contains their first single, "Dorothy," and popular, "What Once Was."
