Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Something To Give Each Other is the third studio album by Australian singer Troye Sivan, released on October 13, 2023, through EMI Music Australia and Capitol Records, as Sivan's first album in five years, following 2018's Bloom. The album was preceded by the singles "Rush", and "Got Me Started", from the lyrics of which the album's title derives. A third single, "One of Your Girls", was released concurrently with the album.
