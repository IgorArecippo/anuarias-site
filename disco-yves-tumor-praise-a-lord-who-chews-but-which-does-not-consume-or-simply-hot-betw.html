Sobre o disco

Praise a Lord Who Chews but Which Does Not Consume; (Or Simply, Hot Between Worlds) is the fifth studio album by American musician Yves Tumor. released on March 17, 2023, through Warp Records. The album features diverse production, blending genres like post-punk, alternative dance, hard rock and electronica. It received widespread critical acclaim, with an average score of 86 out of 100 on Metacritic and a 7.9 out of 10 on AnyDecentMusic?. Critics praised the album's lyrics for addressing themes such as romance, reflection, queerness, and spirituality, and they appreciated Yves Tumor's ability to disrupt hierarchies through their music.
