Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Isolation is the debut studio album by Colombian-American singer Kali Uchis, released worldwide on April 6, 2018, by Rinse Recordings, Virgin Records, and Universal Music Group. The album is supported by five singles: "Tyrant" featuring Jorja Smith, "Nuestro Planeta" featuring Reykon, "After the Storm" featuring Tyler, the Creator and Bootsy Collins, “Dead To Me”, and “Just A Stranger.”
