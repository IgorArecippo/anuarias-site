Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Get Up" is the second EP by South Korean girl group NewJeans (Hangul: 뉴진스), released on July 21, 2023, by ADOR. The project comprises six tracks and was supported by three singles, "Super Shy", "ETA" and "Cool with You", the former of which was released on July 7, 2023, alongside the track "New Jeans". All six tracks received music videos, which attracted attention for featuring collaborations with multiple brands, including the IT company Apple and the media franchise The Powerpuff Girls, and personalities, such as South Korean actress Hoyeon and Hong Kong actor Tony Leung.
