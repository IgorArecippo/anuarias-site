Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Soul Trash" is a mixtape of unreleased Toro y Moi material. Originally released in January of 2019, the mixtape was quickly pulled and later released on streaming services along with a companion film on 29 October 2019. Chaz said that the October release is a higher sound quality than the previous iteration.
