Curiosidades

Do disco

  1. Foi gravado na Mansion, o estúdio de Rick Rubin em Los Angeles.
  2. Murphy citou Bronski Beat, Eurythmics e OMD como inspirações.
  3. É dedicado a Jerry Fuchs, que tocava bateria com a banda em shows e morreu em 2009.
  4. "Home", a última faixa, retoma e suaviza trechos de "Dance Yrself Clean", a primeira.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

The third LCD Soundsystem album to be released May 17th/18th via DFA records. 'Drunk Girls' is the first single to be released from the album. According to Songfacts, This Is Happening was named the top album of 2010 by the Rhapsody music service. "LCD Soundsystem's James Murphy is a consummate student of pop music, and the perfectly executed This Is Happening synthesizes '70s art rock, early '80s avant disco and '90s post-punk without sounding like self-conscious post-modern pastiche, which is no small achievement," observed Rhapsody CEO Sam Chennault.
