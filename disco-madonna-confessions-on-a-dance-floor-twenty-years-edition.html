Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Confessions on a Dance Floor" is the tenth studio album by American singer-songwriter Madonna. It was released on November 9, 2005, by Warner Bros. Records. The album marked a stylistic shift from her previous studio release, "American Life" (2003), and incorporates influences from 1970s disco, 1980s electropop, and contemporary club music. Madonna initially began recording the album with Mirwais Ahmadzaï but later changed creative direction and continued the project with Stuart Price, who had been working with her on the documentary "I'm Going to Tell You a Secret".
