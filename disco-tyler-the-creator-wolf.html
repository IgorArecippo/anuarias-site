Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Wolf is the third studio album by American rapper Tyler, The Creator. It was released on April 2, 2013, by Odd Future Records, distributed by RED Distribution. Wolf is a set in a different direction compared to his other material. Bastard (2009) and Goblin (2011) respectively featured more violent content, while this album features a more production based sound and different themes. The album features guest appearances by Frank Ocean, Mike G, Domo Genesis, Earl Sweatshirt, Left Brain, Hodgy Beats, Erykah Badu, Casey Veggies and Pharrell.
