Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

3.15.20 is the original version of "ATAVISTA", the fourth studio album by Donald Glover, under his stage name Childish Gambino. It was initially uploaded to the website Donaldgloverpresents.com on March 15, 2020, before being taken down 12 hours later; it was thus referred to as Donald Glover Presents. After a countdown, it was then released to streaming and download services under the title 3.15.20 on March 22. It features uncredited guest appearances from Ariana Grande and 21 Savage among others, and also contains the previously released track "Feels Like Summer", referred to as "42.26".
