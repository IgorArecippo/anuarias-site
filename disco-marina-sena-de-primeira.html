Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

De Primeira is the debut solo studio album by Brazilian singer-songwriter Marina Sena. It was released independently on August 19, 2021. The songs on the album were composed by Sena for three years, based on the voice/guitar binomial and, finally, submitted to the Brazilian producer Iuri Rio Branco, who remotely lined up electronic beats and phrasing, which gave Marina's music a flavor that it balances between Latinity, Brazilianness and, above all, modernity.
