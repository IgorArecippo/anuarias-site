Do disco

  1. Fecha a história iniciada em Blurryface (2015), com Trench, Scaled and Icy e Clancy.
  2. É o primeiro disco em que o baterista Josh Dun canta como voz principal, na ponte de "Drum Show".
  3. A última faixa, "Intentions", é "Truce" — última música do disco de 2013, Vessel — tocada de trás pra frente.
  4. Teve a maior venda de vinil numa semana de estreia pra um disco de rock desde 1991.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Breach is the eighth LP and sixth studio album by American musical duo Twenty One Pilots. The album, released on September 12, 2025, is meant to conclude the conceptual series that started with Blurryface and continued through Trench, Scaled and Icy, and Clancy. An alleged tracklist of the album, which included the officially released single, The Contract, was first leaked through Urban Outfitters on June 14th-15th.
