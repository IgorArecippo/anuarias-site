Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Charm" is the third studio album from American singer-songwriter Clairo. Released on July 12, 2024, it is the follow-up to 2021's "Sling", and It is her first album to be self-released, as her previous two were released by Fader and Republic respectively. On May 23, 2024, Clairo announced that a new album, titled Charm and produced by Leon Michels, was scheduled for release on July 12. A press release accompanying the announcement of Charm described the album as "a collection of warm, '70s-inspired grooves that move lithely between jazz, psychedelic folk and soul".
