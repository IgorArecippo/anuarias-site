Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Me Chama de Gato Que Eu Sou Sua is the third studio album by Brazilian singer-songwriter Ana Frango Elétrico, released on October 20, 2023, via Risco. The album starts from an attentive listening to Brazilian musical production between the 70s and 80s, pointing to the work of industry veterans, mainly characters who worked behind the scenes, but at no point does it leave aside poetic vulnerability. These are compositions that highlight the strength of feelings, strengthen bonds and reverberate beyond any technical quality.
