Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

ORQUÍDEAS is the fourth studio album and second Spanish-language album by Kali Uchis, released on January 12, 2024, through Geffen Records. It features guest appearances by Peso Pluma, El Alfa, JT, KAROL G and Rauw Alejandro. The album debuted at number two on the US Billboard 200, marking Uchis' highest-charting album. The album was announced following Uchis' reveal in 2022 that she had two ready-to-release albums, one in English and one in Spanish.
