Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Sacred Hearts Club" is the third studio album by American indie pop band Foster the People. It was released on July 21, 2017, by Columbia Records. Prior to its release, the band put out an EP, III, featuring the tracks Pay the Man, Doing It for the Money, and SHC.
