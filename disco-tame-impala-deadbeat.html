Do disco

  1. É inspirado na cultura das "bush doofs", festas eletrônicas no mato, e na cena rave da Austrália Ocidental.
  2. Foi terminado em Fremantle e na Wave House, a casa de praia onde Parker gravou o primeiro disco.
  3. É o primeiro disco do Tame Impala pela Columbia; Joe Keery, o Djo, aparece no clipe de "Loser".
  4. "End of Summer" ganhou o Grammy de Melhor Gravação Dance/Eletrônica.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Deadbeat is the fifth studio album by Australian psychedelic music project Tame Impala, released on October 17, 2025, serving as the follow-up to the 2020 album The Slow Rush. The album is set to explore a more club-oriented, experimental sound described as “rave psychedelic,” while maintaining Tame Impala’s signature melodic and textural approach. Background and Release On July 18, 2025, Tame Impala shared an unnamed 12” vinyl record available for pre-order, which was speculated to be related to his upcoming fifth album.
