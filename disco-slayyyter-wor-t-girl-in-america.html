Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"WOR$T GIRL IN AMERICA" is the third studio album by American singer Slayyyter. It was released on March 27, 2026. The album is inspired by Slayyyter's upbringing in St. Louis and characters from her adolescence. The artist described it as a "portrait of a woman from the Midwest". The album was supported by the release of five singles and topped the US Top Dance Albums chart. In comparison to her earlier releases "Troubled Paradise" and "Starfucker", Slayyyter described the album as more authentically her and one where she tried revisiting the formative sounds from her teenage years.
