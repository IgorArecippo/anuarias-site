Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

SCARING THE HOES is a collaborative studio album by American rappers JPEGMAFIA and Danny Brown. It was released on March 24, 2023. Prior to the release, JPEGMAFIA and Danny Brown had collaborated on various singles, Many times through 2022 and 2023, both JPEGMAFIA and Danny Brown shared pics of them together in the studio. SCARING THE HOES was supported by the singles "Lean Beef Patty" and "SCARING THE HOES".
