Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Dev Hynes has been kicking around the indie-sphere for the better part of a decade, leading disparate projects like Test Icicles, Lightspeed Champion, and, most recently, the woozy and Princely bedroom-R&B alter-ego Blood Orange. Lately, though, he’s high-stepped hard into the zeitgeist, leading major-label refugee collaborators like Solange and Sky Ferreira to breezy cool-pop stardom. And later this fall, he’ll release Cupid Deluxe, his second Blood Orange album and his first since his production helped change the sound of the indie mainstream.
