Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

“Blue Banisters” is the eighth studio album by American singer-songwriter Lana Del Rey. It was released on October 22, 2021 as the follow-up to her seventh studio album “Chemtrails Over The Country Club” (2021), and was published through Interscope and Polydor Records. The album was produced by Del Rey, Zachary Dawes, Loren Humphrey, Mike Dean, Barrie-James O'Neill, and Rick Nowels. The album artwork that includes 2 alternate covers includes photos shot by previous collaborator, Neil Krug.
