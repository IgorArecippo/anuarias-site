Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Visions Of A Life" is the second studio album by British alternative-rock band Wolf Alice. It was released on the 29th September 2017 through the label Dirty Hit. The album received acclaim from music critics and appeared on several year-end lists, as well as winning the 2018 Mercury Prize.
