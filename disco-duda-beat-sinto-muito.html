Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Sinto Muito is the debut studio album by Brazilian singer and songwriter Duda Beat, released on April 27, 2018. "Bixinho" was released as the album's lead single, followed by "Derretendo" and "Bolo de Rolo".
