Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Heaven to a Tortured Mind is the fourth studio album by American musician Yves Tumor, released on April 3, 2020, via Warp Records. The album received widespread critical acclaim, with an average score of 88 on Metacritic, indicating "universal acclaim". Critics praised its psychedelic soul sound and considered it a defining work by Yves Tumor. They commended the album's coherence, conciseness and artistic ambition, describing it as a gratifying and intense record that showcases Yves Tumor as a significant figure in contemporary music.
