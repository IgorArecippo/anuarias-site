Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

moisturizer is the second studio album by British rock band Wet Leg, released on 11 July 2025 by Domino Recording Company. It was first teased on Instagram by the band with a photo of the band sat on and around a sofa with the caption "we're so back" and a link to their website. Isolated in a remote house in the countryside, the record was written in a creative frenzy, diving into themes of obsession and all-consuming love. While their 2022 debut earned Grammy wins and chart-topping success, Moisturizer brings the bite: brash guitars, heavy beats, and a fearless devotion…
