Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Scarlet" is the fourth studio album by American rapper and singer Doja Cat, released on September 22, 2023, via Kemosabe and RCA Records. Disillusioned with pop music and dissatisfied with music critics questioning her status as a rapper, Doja felt inspired to create a "masculine" follow-up to her third studio album, "Planet Her". Musically, the record marks a departure from the pop-driven sounds of its predecessor, being predominantly centered around the genres of hip hop and R&B.
