Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"brat and it's the same but there's three more songs so it's not" is the deluxe version of Charli XCX's sixth studio album, BRAT. It was released on June 10, 2024, via Atlantic Records, and features three additional tracks, "Hello Goodbye", "Guess" and "Spring breakers".
