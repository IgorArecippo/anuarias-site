Do disco

  1. É uma mixtape de uns 20 minutos, inspirada na dance music clássica de Basement Jaxx, Groove Armada e Fatboy Slim.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Fancy That marks PinkPantheress’ second mixtape alongside her debut mixtape to hell with it. The mixtape was first hinted at over the course of March 2025 when her official website was changed to a different theme. Additionally, during an interview with Mixmag, she describes her new era behind this mixtape and also compares how different her fanbase was during the release of to hell with it and Heaven knows up until now.
