Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Diretoria is the second EP by Brazilian hip hop duo Tasha & Tracie. It was released on August 19, 2021, via Ceia Ent. The project's name, in addition to referring to the structure of samba schools, is a reference to the homonymous song by Brazilian singer MC Primo, one of the six MC's from the Baixada Santista who were murdered between 2010 and 2013.
