Do disco

  1. Antes de ser Ctrl, o disco se chamava A.
  2. SZA disse que o disco é sobre controle: "faltou controle a vida toda, e eu desejei isso a vida toda".
  3. Rick Rubin a ensinou a cortar excessos: "quanto mais você tira, mais espaço cria pro resto ficar bonito".
  4. "Love Galore", com Travis Scott, foi feita no escritório da mãe do produtor Carter Lang, em Michigan.
  5. Com o disco atrasando anos, SZA chegou a dizer publicamente que ia largar a música.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Ctrl is the debut studio album by American singer and songwriter SZA. The album was released on June 9, 2017, through Top Dawg Entertainment, and features guest appearances from Kendrick Lamar, Travis Scott, Isaiah Rashad and James Fauntleroy. Ctrl received widespread critical acclaim upon its release, with many praising SZA's distinctive and soulful voice, as well as her introspective and confessional lyrics. The album was praised for its honesty and vulnerability, and for its exploration of themes such as love, heartbreak, and self-acceptance.
