Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

SATURATION II is the second studio album by American boy band BROCKHAMPTON, released on August 25, 2017. Production is primarily handled by Romil Hemnani, alongside production duo Q3, bearface, Kevin Abstract and JOBA. SATURATION II is the follow-up to BROCKHAMPTON’s debut album SATURATION. The album was supported by four singles, "GUMMY", "SWAMP", "JUNKY" and "SWEET". The lead single, "GUMMY" was released on August 2, 2017. The second single, "SWAMP" was released on August 8, 2017.
