Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

ALL AMERICAN TRASH is the only mixtape by BROCKHAMPTON, released on March 30, 2016, following the release of its only single, FLIP MO. This mixtape also would feature the only major appearances of Rodney Tenor and Albert Gordon, who would both leave the group prior to the release of the SATURATION trilogy in 2017. A documentary would also release in April 2016, chronicling the project's development.
