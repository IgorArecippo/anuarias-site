Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Norman Fucking Rockwell! is the sixth studio album by Lana Del Rey, released on August 30, 2019, via Interscope and Polydor Records. It features a soft rock sound with psych-rock songs and piano ballads, and includes references to various classic rock artists. The title is a nod to painter and illustrator Norman Rockwell. To promote the album, five singles were released: "Mariners Apartment Complex", "Venice Bitch", "hope is a dangerous thing for a woman like me to have - but i have it", "Doin' Time" and "The greatest".
