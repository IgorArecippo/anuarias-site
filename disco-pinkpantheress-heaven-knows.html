Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Heaven Knows" is the debut studio album by English singer-songwriter and record producer PinkPantheress, released through Warner UK on 10 November 2023. The album follows her 2021 mixtape "To Hell with It" and features the singles "Boy's a Liar Pt. 2" with Ice Spice, "Mosquito", "Capable of Love", and "Nice to Meet You" featuring Central Cee. A remix album of "Heaven Knows" was released on 7 December 2023 through digital download; a planned CD release was reportedly cancelled for unspecified reasons.
