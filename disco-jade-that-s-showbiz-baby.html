Sobre o disco

That's Showbiz Baby! is the high-anticipated debut solo studio album by English singer JADE. It was released on 12 September 2025 through RCA Records, marking a major artistic turning point for the former Little Mix member as she officially launched her solo career. The album represents a bold reintroduction of JADE as an individual artist, embracing theatrical pop, disco influences, emotional storytelling and experimental production.
