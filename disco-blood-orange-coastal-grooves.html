Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Coastal Grooves is the debut studio album by Blood Orange, released on August 30, 2011, via Domino Recording Company. The album incorporates stylistic elements of indie rock, baroque pop, folk, contemporary R&B, jazz, soul and electronic music. By fusing many of these elements together, Blood Orange captures sounds of grooviness, reverbed guitar lines, and piano synths to create music that is described as being a part of a post-punk atmosphere.
