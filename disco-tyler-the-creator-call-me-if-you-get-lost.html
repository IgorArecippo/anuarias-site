Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

CALL ME IF YOU GET LOST is the seventh studio album by American rapper, singer, songwriter and producer Tyler, The Creator. The album was released on June 25, 2021, through A Boy Is a Gun and Columbia Records. The track LUMBERJACK was released as a single on June 16, and WUSYANAME (feat. Youngboy Never Broke Again & Ty Dolla $ign) was teased via music video on June 22. The tracks RISE! (feat. DAISY WORLD), MOMMA TALK, SIR BAUDELAIRE (feat. DJ Drama), and various other unnamed samples were also teased through voicemail, where fans could dial the phone number "+1 (855)
