Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

The Family is the seventh and penultimate studio album by American boy band BROCKHAMPTON. The album was officially released on November 17, 2022, through RCA and Question Everything. The release of a final album in 2022 was announced during the band's performance at the 2022 Coachella festival. The group surprised fans with an announcement of an additional album TM the day of The Family's release, to be released the following day on November 18, 2022.
