Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

“Lust for Life” is the fifth studio album by American singer-songwriter Lana Del Rey, released on July 21, 2017, through Polydor Records and Interscope Records. Marking a return to the "hip-hop inspired" sound of her major-label debut, Lust for Life features production from past collaborators Rick Nowels, Kieron Menzies and Emile Haynie, while also working for the first time with producers Boi-1da, Max Martin, Benny Blanco and Metro Boomin.
