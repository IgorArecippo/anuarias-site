Do disco

  1. O título tem sentido triplo: a herança ganense de Amaarae, a identidade dela e as raízes negras da música eletrônica que inspirou o disco.

  3. O clipe de "SMO" mistura referências de Donna Summer ("Love to Love You Baby") e da Janet Jackson da era Control.
  4. A Pitchfork deu 8,8 e o selo de Best New Music.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

BLACK STAR is the third studio album by the Ghanaian-American singer Amaarae. It was released on August 8, 2025, through Golden Child Entertainment and Interscope Records. The album follows her critically acclaimed 2023 record Fountain Baby and takes a more playful approach, drawing inspiration from Ghanaian highlife, baile funk and dance music rooted in Black cultural heritage.
