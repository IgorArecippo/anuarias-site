Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

CAPRISONGS is the first mixtape by FKA twigs. It was released on January 14, 2022, through Young and Atlantic Records. The album record features guest appearances from Pa Salieu, The Weeknd, Shygirl, Dystopia, Rema, Daniel Caesar, Jorja Smith and Unknown T. The production is handled by El Guincho, who co-executive produced the mixtape alongside twigs, as well as Arca, Cirkut and Mike Dean, among others. The mixtape was supported by two singles, "tears in the club" and "jealousy", which feature The Weeknd and Rema, and were released on December 16, 2021 and January 13, 2022.
