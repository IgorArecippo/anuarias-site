Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

What's Your Pleasure? is the fourth studio album by English singer-songwriter Jessie Ware, released on June 26, 2020, via PMR Records and Virgin EMI Records. Ware described the album as a "two-year labour of love", further citing the album as her visions of "escapism groove". The twelve tracks on What's Your Pleasure? move away from her usual "melancholy" sound in her previous releases, to focus on disco, hi-NRG and house. A reissue subtitled What's Your Pleasure? (The Platinum Pleasure Edition), was released on June 11, 2021 and preceded by the singles "Please" and "Hot n Heavy".
