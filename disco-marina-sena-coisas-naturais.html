Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Coisas Naturais (English: Natural Things) is the third studio album by Brazilian singer Marina Sena, released on 31 March 2025 through Sony Music Brasil. It was announced on 24 March 2025 through Marina's social media, along with the pre-save of the record. The album cover was revealed on 26 March 2025. Its lead single "Numa Ilha" was released on 4 December 2024. The album features international collaborations, including artists Gaia from Italy and Nenny from Portugal, who bring European influences. Spanish-based band Çantamarta brings a Latin Caribbean take on reggaeton to the project.
