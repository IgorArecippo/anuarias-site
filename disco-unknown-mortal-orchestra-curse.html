Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

CURSE is an EP by New Zealand band Unknown Mortal Orchestra. It was announced on May 28, 2025, along with the release of the first and only single from the EP, "Boys With the Characteristics of Wolves." Two months after the most previous release by the band, IC-02 Bogotá , singer Ruban Nielson announced the release of a new song, Boys With the Characteristics of Wolves, along with a new EP, Curse, to be released on June 18.
