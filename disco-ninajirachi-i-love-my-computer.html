Do disco

  1. É uma homenagem ao computador dela, que ela chama de "o maior colaborador que vou conhecer nesta vida".

  4. Ninajirachi já tinha feito o projeto de demonstração oficial do Ableton Live 11, o programa em que produz.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

I Love My Computer is the debut album from Australian electronic producer Ninajirachi, released on August 8, 2025. On June 12, 2025, the album was formally announced through her social media accounts. The album’s lead single, “All I Am” was released on February 25, 2025 and marked the beginning of a brand new era of Ninajirachi. Existing within the same universe as her previous release and her newly coined self-referential music style, girlEDM, this new computer-obsessed era was first teased through an interactive website, 4562—curated by Ninajirachi and frequent collaborator Aria Zarzycki.
