Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Big Fish Theory is the second full-length album by American hip-hop artist Vince Staples. The album received widespread acclaim from critics and debuted at number 16 on the US Billboard 200. Featuring an avant-garde style that leans toward electronic club music genres such as house and Detroit techno, it contains production work from Zack Sekoff, Ray Brady, and Justin Vernon, among others; as well as vocal contributions from a variety of artists including Kilo Kish, Kendrick Lamar, and A$AP Rocky.
