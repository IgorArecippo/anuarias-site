Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Sister is the sixth studio album by American pop duo Frost Children. It was released on September 12, 2025, via True Panther Sounds and Dirty Hit in vinyl, CD, cassette and digital formats. It includes four singles released in mid-2025, including "Control", "Falling", "What Is Forever For", and "Bound2U". Preceded by the duo's 2023 release, Hearth Room, the album consists of fourteen tracks ranging between two and four minutes each, excluding "2 Løve", whose runtime exceeds five minutes.
