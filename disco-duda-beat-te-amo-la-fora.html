Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Te Amo Lá Fora is the second studio album by Brazilian singer-songwriter Duda Beat, released on April 27, 2021. "Meu Pisêro" was released as the lead single from the album on March 13, 2021. The singer announced the album on April 21, 2021, along with the album cover.
