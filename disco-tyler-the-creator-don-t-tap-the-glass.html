Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Don't Tap the Glass" is the ninth studio album by American rapper and producer Tyler, the Creator, released through Columbia Records on July 21, 2025. The album features guest appearances from Pharrell Williams (credited under both his name and his alter ego, Sk8brd), Madison McFerrin, and Yebba. Tyler served as the sole producer for all ten tracks on the album. The album was promoted with two singles, "Ring Ring Ring" and "Sugar on My Tongue", which were released to US rhythmic radio on July 30 and August 20, respectively.
