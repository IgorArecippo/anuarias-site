Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Live at SoFi Stadium is the first live album by Canadian singer–songwriter the Weeknd. It was announced on March 1, 2023, and released the following evening on March 2, 2023, in North America and on March 3, 2023, in the rest of the world, through XO and Republic Records.
