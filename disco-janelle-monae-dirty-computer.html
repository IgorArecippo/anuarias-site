Curiosidades

Do disco

  1. Prince trabalhou no disco com Janelle antes de morrer; o groove de sintetizador de "Make Me Feel" seria dele, e já tinha tocado numa festa sua anos antes.
  2. É o primeiro disco dela sem a androide Cindi Mayweather: Janelle disse que se esconder em metáforas era mais seguro que mostrar quem ela era.
  3. Ela pausou o disco pra atuar em Moonlight e Estrelas Além do Tempo.
  4. Veio acompanhado de um filme de 46 minutos, um "emotion picture", com Tessa Thompson; o teaser passou nos cinemas antes de Pantera Negra.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Dirty Computer is the third studio album by American recording artist Janelle Monáe, released on April 27, 2018 by Wondaland Arts Society, Epic Records and Sony Music. It is the follow up to her critically acclaimed studio albums, The ArchAndroid (2010) and The Electric Lady (2013). The album received universal critical acclaim upon release; it was included in the top three of seven publications' Best Album of 2018 lists, and received two nominations at the 61st Annual Grammy Awards, including Album of the Year. It debuted at number six on the Billboard 200 and was further promoted by Monae's Dirty Computer Tour.
