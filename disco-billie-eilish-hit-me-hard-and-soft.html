Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

HIT ME HARD AND SOFT is the third studio album by American singer and songwriter Billie Eilish, released on 17 May 2024 through Darkroom and Interscope Records. The album was co-written with her brother and frequent collaborator FINNEAS, who also produced the record. Musically, it has been described as alternative pop and bedroom pop. The album received widespread acclaim from critics, who praised its production, songwriting, and vocal performances.
