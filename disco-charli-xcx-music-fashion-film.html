Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

“Music, Fashion, Film” is the eighth studio album by the British singer and songwriter Charli xcx, released on 24 July 2026. The album follows her previous album "BRAT" and her soundtrack album " Wuthering Heights". Charli said she wasn't thinking on making new music after Wuthering Heights, but then one day she had the idea of "Music, Fashion, Film". The album gathers reflections on contemporary art through the three art forms that, in her opinion, most influence society: music, fashion, and film.
