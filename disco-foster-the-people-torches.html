Do disco

  1. Mark Foster gravou "Pumped Up Kicks" quando trabalhava compondo jingles; a música viralizou e o disco foi feito pra "sustentar" o sucesso dela.
  2. A capa foi desenhada por um amigo, Young & Sick; o baterista era o único contra, mas os personagens viraram um universo usado em shows e produtos.
  3. Kenny G tocou com a banda em "Houdini" no Saturday Night Live.
  4. The Edge, do U2, e Elton John citaram Torches entre os discos favoritos de 2011.

Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Torches is the debut album from Indie pop band Foster the People. It was first released on 23 May 2011 through Columbia Records and Startime, International in the United States. The album was preceded by the release of the single "Pumped Up Kicks", which debuted at number 73 on the Billboard Hot 100. Upon release, the track "Helena Beat" was selected as Single of the Week on iTunes; thus making it a free download. Torches is available on iTunes with the bonus track "Broken Jaw".
