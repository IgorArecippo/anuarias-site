Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"Addison" is the debut studio album by American singer Addison Rae, released on June 6, 2025, through Columbia Records under exclusive licensing from As Long As I'm Dancing LLC. The album was produced by Elvira Anderfjärd and Luka Kloser, who also co-wrote all of its songs with Rae. It was supported by the singles "Diet Pepsi", "Aquamarine", "High Fashion", "Headphones On" and "Fame Is a Gun". "Addison" received generally favourable reviews from music critics, with an average score of 79 on Metacritic based on 14 reviews.
