Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"CA$INO" is the sophmore studio album by American rapper and producer Baby Keem, released on February 20, 2026. The album features Kendrick Lamar, Momo Boyd, Too $hort, & Che Ecru. After a 4 year-long wait, the highly anticipated project was announced on February 11 via both Keem's Instagram and the release of the first part of a three part documentary entitled 'Booman' on Youtube. An hour before the album's release, Keem hosted a listening party that was livestreamed on Youtube.
