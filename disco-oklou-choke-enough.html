Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

choke enough is the debut studio album by French musician Oklou, released on February 7, 2025, through True Panther Sounds. The album features collaborations with artists such as Bladee and underscores. Notable tracks include "family and friends," "harvest sky," the title track "choke enough," "take me by the hand," and "blade bird."
