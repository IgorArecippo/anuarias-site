Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Like..? is the debut EP by American rapper Ice Spice. It was released on January 20, 2023 through 10K Projects and Capitol Records, and was announced only hours prior to its release. The EP was preceded by the singles; "Munch (Feelin’ U)", "Bikini Bottom" and "In Ha Mood". Frequent collaborator RiotUSA served as the executive producer for the EP.
