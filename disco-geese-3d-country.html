Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

3D Country is the third studio album by American indie rock band Geese, released on June 23, 2023, through Partisan Records and Play It Again Sam. Produced by James Ford, the album was preceded by the singles "Cowboy Nudes", "Mysterious Love", "3D Country", and "I See Myself", and received favorable reviews from critics. It is the band's final album to feature founding guitarist Foster Hudson, who departed from the band in December 2023 to focus on his academic studies. A follow-up EP, 4D Country, was released in October 2023 featuring four additional tracks from the album's recording sessions.
