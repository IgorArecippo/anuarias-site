Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

A Tábua de Esmeralda is the eleventh studio album by Brazilian singer Jorge Ben, released on LP in 1974. The album is notable for being the last project in which Jorge Ben made intensive use of the acoustic guitar. The instrument was later abandoned in 1976 due to technical reasons that, in Ben’s own words, made it difficult to reproduce the same sound quality: "The guitar pickup we used no longer exists.
