Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Inspired by her summer tour of the same name, "MARINADA VOL. 1" is the third EP by Brazilian singer-songwriter Marina Sena, released on Dec 18, 2025, by Sony Music Brazil. Inspired by the tropical climate of our country, the project promises to translate into music the heat, the body, and the movement typical of the most anticipated season of the year, inviting the public to feel, dance, and intensely experience the period. It echoes the proposal of "immersion in summer": just as a marinade gives flavor and intensity to the ingredients, here Marina seeks to envelop the listener in warmth,…
