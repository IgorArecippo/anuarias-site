Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

American Dream (stylized on digital releases as american dream) is the fourth studio album by American rock band LCD Soundsystem. It was released on September 1, 2017 through DFA Records and Columbia Records. It was first announced on January 5, 2016, the day after it was revealed that the band was reuniting after a disbandment lasting nearly five years. American Dream acts as the band's first album in seven years, following This Is Happening from 2010.
