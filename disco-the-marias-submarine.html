Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Submarine is the second studio album by the American band The Marías, released on May 31, 2024 through Atlantic Records. The album features songs in English and Spanish and was produced entirely by the band itself. This album marks a new beginning in the same immersive style that the band is used to in their songs and as in their predecessor album “CINEMA”. Submarine presents immersive waves in its melodies as if we were underwater, along with its melancholic and progressively calm cathartic lyrics makes it very charming and hypnotic to listen to.
