Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Rito de Passá is the debut album by Brazilian singer-songwriter Mc Tha, released on June 21, 2019, on the Elemess label, by the Pará artist Jaloo, who also produced the album. Strongly influenced by umbanda, funk and R&B, Mc Tha mixes her roots in all the tracks on the album, making each fragment reveal itself to the public in small doses, as a precious presentation exercise.
