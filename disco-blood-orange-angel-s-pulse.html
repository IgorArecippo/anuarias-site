Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Angel's Pulse is a mixtape by Dev Hynes as Blood Orange. It was released via Domino Recording Company on 12 July 2019. It is his first release since his fourth studio album Negro Swan. Originally released digitally, it was later released on compact disc, vinyl, and cassette tape on 4 October 2019. At The Late Late Show with James Corden on 16 April 2019, Dev Hynes performed "Something to Do" and "Dark & Handsome" for the first time. A music video for "Benzo" was released on 15 July 2019.
