Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

111 Deluxe (stylized in all caps) is the third remix album by Brazilian singer and drag queen Pabllo Vittar. It was released on November 27, 2020, through Sony Music Brazil. With two new songs: "Bandida", featuring Pocah, and "Eu Vou" and five more remixes, in which they are present: Jaloo, Alice Glass, Biu do Piseiro, Getúlio Abelha and other not-well-known artists, Pabllo Vittar once again ventures into new rhythms and does it in a magnificent way. Mixing rhythms that are on the rise, such as piseiro, in the Northeast, and funk, the singer shows once again that she knows how…
