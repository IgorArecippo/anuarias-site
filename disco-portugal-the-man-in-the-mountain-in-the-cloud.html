Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

"In the Mountain in the Cloud" is the sixth album by Portugal. The Man. It was released in 2011 as the bands major label debut. Supported by the singles Got It All and So American, it got good reviews and became their first album to chart on the Billboard 200 (peaking at 42nd).
