Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Chris Black Changed My Life is the ninth studio album by American rock band Portugal. The Man. It was released on June 23, 2023, by Atlantic Records. The album was primarily produced by Jeff Bhasker and Asa Taccone, with Tommy Danvers, Tyler Johnson, and Casey Bates also contributing to production. The album is dedicated to the band's longtime friend and the album's namesake Chris Black, who died in May 2019.
