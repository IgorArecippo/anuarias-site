Sobre o disco

em inglês · traduzir ↗

Take me home is the first EP by PinkPantheress, released on December 16, 2022, via Warner Records. The three-track project follows her 2021 mixtape to hell with it and comes ahead of her debut studio album Heaven knows. Before its release, PinkPantheress described the EP as "a Christmas gift for anyone in need of some upbeat music". The EP features production from Future Cut, Phil, KAYTRANADA, and others, blending PinkPantheress' signature alt-pop and drum and bass influences with polished, danceable beats.
